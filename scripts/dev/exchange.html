FAQ

  • How does the Crypto-exchanger work?

    Online cryptocurrency exchanger Trustee Plus works in real time - all exchange rates are up to date at the time of exchange, and transactions are completed in minutes.

  • Where to exchange cryptocurrency profitably?

    There are many platforms for currency exchange. Trustee Plus is a service that selects the most favorable offer, but the right to choose is always left to the user.

  • Are there restrictions on the transaction amount — the minimum amount of exchange?

    Each cryptocurrency has its own limits on the minimum transaction amount.

  • At what point is the transaction rate fixed?

    The transaction rate for cryptocurrency transactions in Trustee Plus is fixed at the moment of the transaction.

  • Will I be charged additional commissions when exchanging?

    There are no commissions for cryptocurrency transactions in Trustee Plus, only network commissions.

  • How long does it take to exchange?

    Fast exchanges are one of the key advantages of Trustee Plus. Transactions are usually completed in a matter of minutes.
